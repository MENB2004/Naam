# 25. NAAM (നാം) — Maintenance and Future Scope

## 1. Post-MVP Roadmap
1. **IoT Water Quality Sensor Integration:** Ingest real-time pH, turbidity, and dissolved oxygen telemetry from solar-powered river buoys along the Periyar and Bharathappuzha rivers.
2. **Rainfall & Flood Warning Integration:** Automated integration with Kerala State Disaster Management Authority (KSDMA) rainfall feeds to generate automated flood risk advisories.
3. **Automated Haritha Karma Sena Routing:** AI-assisted route optimization for waste collection vehicles based on geo-clustered complaint density.
4. **Community Green Rewards:** Citizen reward points for verified environmental stewardship and waste segregation reporting.
